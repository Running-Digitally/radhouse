The live Radhouse page read as a technical status document: six dense sections
and a link list, without a clear interactive introduction. The refresh keeps
its domestic green/cream identity and original mark, introducing a small
model house with working hinges, inset doors, brass fittings, an enamel sign,
layered paper and a timber base. The doors open on one illustrated assignment.
The three stages explain an assignment, retained work and a person's review.
They are labelled as an illustration, not a live agent or product screenshot.

There is one opening control and one tab group. Only the current paper is
accessible. A coordinated door movement and a short paper arrival respond to
explicit action; there is no autoplay, continuous ornamental motion or drag
instruction. Reserved controls keep the surrounding page stable.
On a narrow screen, a pointer opening brings the example into view; keyboard
opening retains normal focus order. Door swings are clipped at the viewport
edge during resize transitions.
The remaining page distinguishes the person using the agent from the person running its
computer, then separates checked pilot work from outstanding release work.
Technical evidence and the roadmap are native disclosures. GitHub is the
follow-up route.
